About Us

We Take Pride
in Every Cut.

Blank Canvas Restorations & Carpentry was built on a simple belief: every home deserves work done right. We're a Calgary-based team of skilled craftsmen who care deeply about the quality of what we build and restore.

We work primarily with residential clients who value craftsmanship over quick fixes — people who want work that will still look excellent in 20 years.

Every project starts as a blank canvas. Our job is to make it something worth keeping.
